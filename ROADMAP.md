## Rules

- **3 hours of focused study a day, Monday to Saturday.** Sunday is rest, spaced repetition (`/study`) and catch-up.
- A day = recall 15 · probes 30 · katas 95 · break 10 · gate and journal 30 (minutes). `/study` runs it; `scripts/run.sh <ID>` shows your katas.
- A task is **done** only when the review passes: automated gate green + rubric score ≥ 80 + no blocker. See [`curriculum/README.md`](curriculum/README.md).
- A late finish or a failed review slips every later row by the same number of study days.
- Each stage ends with a **gate day**: closed-book exam plus catch-up.
